# Security policy

These are the release artefacts for Softspoken ([softspoken.io](https://softspoken.io)): the
installers and update manifests the app itself pulls from. Only the **latest release** is
supported; the app auto-updates by default, so most installs are current.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@softspoken.io** with what you found, how to reproduce it, and its impact. Don't
include secrets or other users' data in the report itself. We'll acknowledge within **2 business
days** and keep you posted while we work on it.

Our full security policy and posture (for IT and security reviewers) is published at
[softspoken.io/security](https://softspoken.io/security).
